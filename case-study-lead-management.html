The challenge

Good leads can get lost in everyday work.

Inquiries arrive through email, phone, website forms, and referrals. Without a clear system, small-business owners can lose track of who needs a response, which conversations are progressing, and what to do next.

Not every business needs an expensive CRM. Sometimes a simple, consistent workflow is the better starting point.

The project goal

Bring lead details into one place, make follow-up priorities visible, track sales stages, and provide an easy-to-read dashboard that a small business can use without a recurring CRM subscription.

The solution

One workbook. The essentials built in.

The XELQO demonstration includes a customizable lead tracker, formula-driven dashboard, and quick-start guide.

01 / ORGANIZE

Centralized lead tracker

Record contact information, service interest, lead source, estimated value, and next actions.

02 / PRIORITIZE

Follow-up alerts

Highlight overdue follow-ups, tasks due today, upcoming dates, and missing follow-up dates.

03 / TRACK

Consistent sales stages

Use dropdowns for New, Contacted, Discovery, Proposal Sent, Won, Lost, and On Hold.

04 / REVIEW

Pipeline dashboard

See lead counts, open opportunities, proposals, won deals, and follow-up activity.

05 / ESTIMATE

Opportunity visibility

View estimated values and an illustrative weighted pipeline based on sales stage.

06 / ADOPT

Quick-start guidance

Clear instructions explain how to enter leads, update stages, and review progress.

Everyday workflow

From inquiry to next step.

STEP 01

Capture

Enter a new inquiry into the tracker.

STEP 02

Qualify

Assign service, status, priority, and value.

STEP 03

Follow up

Set a date and specific next action.

STEP 04

Review

Check the dashboard and outstanding tasks.

What this demonstrates

Better visibility without unnecessary complexity.

The working spreadsheet demonstrates how a small business can structure lead information and surface follow-up priorities using familiar tools.

Project transparency: This is an independent XELQO demonstration, not a commissioned client project. No revenue growth, time savings, or conversion improvement is claimed.

Built with practical tools

Google Sheets: structured tracker, dropdowns, dashboard.

Spreadsheet formulas: follow-up flags, pipeline counts, estimates.

Documentation: quick-start guide for daily use.

Important scope note

New inquiries are entered manually. Website forms and email do not automatically sync into this tracker; advanced CRM integrations would be separate work.
