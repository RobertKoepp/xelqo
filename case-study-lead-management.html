The challenge

Good leads can get lost in everyday work.

For a small business, inquiries may arrive by email, phone, website forms, and referrals. Without a consistent process, it's difficult to remember who needs a response, which conversations are progressing, and what to do next.

Many owners don't need an expensive CRM to get started. They need a straightforward system they can actually use.

The goal

  • Keep lead details in one organized place.
  • Make the next follow-up action visible.
  • Track each opportunity from inquiry to outcome.
  • See a simple summary of pipeline activity.
  • Use familiar tools without a recurring CRM subscription.
The solution

A simple system with the essentials built in.

XELQO designed a customizable Google Sheets workbook with a lead tracker, live summary dashboard, and quick-start guide.

01 / ORGANIZE

One lead tracker

Record business names, contact information, service interest, source, estimated value, and the next action in a consistent format.

02 / PRIORITIZE

Follow-up alerts

Formula-driven labels flag overdue items, follow-ups due today, upcoming dates, and leads missing a follow-up date.

03 / TRACK

Clear sales stages

Dropdowns keep pipeline statuses consistent, from New and Contacted to Discovery, Proposal Sent, Won, Lost, and On Hold.

04 / REVIEW

Pipeline dashboard

See lead counts, open opportunities, proposals, won deals, and follow-up activity without manually tallying rows.

05 / PLAN

Opportunity estimates

Track estimated deal values and view an illustrative weighted pipeline based on each opportunity's stage.

06 / ADOPT

Easy handoff

A built-in quick-start guide explains how to enter leads, update stages, and review the dashboard.

Everyday workflow

From first inquiry to next step.

STEP 01

Capture

Enter the new inquiry into the lead tracker.

STEP 02

Qualify

Assign a service, status, priority, and estimated value.

STEP 03

Follow up

Set a follow-up date and clear next action.

STEP 04

Review

Use the dashboard to spot overdue tasks and pipeline changes.
